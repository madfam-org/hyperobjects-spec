Term

strap slot

Definition

The rectangular opening through a piece of solid hardware that a strap or webbing passes through, together with the garment edge that passes through it. Buckles, clips and sliders are made almost entirely of this feature: the slot is the whole reason the object exists, and its width is a garment measurement wearing hardware clothing.

Termo

passador de fita

Definição

A abertura retangular de uma peça de ferragem sólida pela qual passa uma fita ou correia, juntamente com o bordo de peça que a atravessa. Fivelas, clipes e reguladores são feitos quase inteiramente deste detalhe: o passador é toda a razão de ser do objeto, e a sua largura é uma medida de vestuário vestida de ferragem.

Restricción

One parameter drives both sides or the strap does not thread. The garment's finished strap width and the hardware's slot width must come from the same number plus a declared clearance — fashion-cabinet/garter-belt maps `strap_w` to the clip's `strap_w` with `grip_clear: 0.3`, and a bridge that maps neither is two objects agreeing only by name.
