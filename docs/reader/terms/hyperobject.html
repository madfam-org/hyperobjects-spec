Term

hyperobject

Definition

In this commons, an object that is never one artifact but the family it regenerates into — a region of parameter space, sliced per render, real through the contracts that validate it. The word is borrowed twice: from Timothy Morton, whose hyperobjects are entities too vast to point at, scaled down here to the workbench; and from computing, where hyper- marks the thing that generates things, as in hypertext and hyperparameter. Neither borrowing is a claim to metaphysics.
