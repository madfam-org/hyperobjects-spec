Term

side seam

Definition

The seam joining the front of a garment to its back down the side of the body, and the shaping that lives in it. It is where a draft puts the difference between a bust girth and a waist girth, because it is the one vertical line on a simple garment that can be curved without showing as a design feature.

Termo

costura lateral

Definição

A costura que une a frente de uma peça às costas pelo lado do corpo, e o entalhe que nela vive. É onde um traçado coloca a diferença entre um perímetro de busto e um de cintura, porque é a única linha vertical de uma peça simples que pode ser curvada sem se ler como um recurso de desenho.

Restricción

The two edges are sewn to each other, so they are one length: front side and back side must measure the same from underarm to hem, and shaping taken from only one of them is shaping that will not close.

Notas

A garment can legitimately have no side seam — the cut-in-one and uncut-cloth traditions run the body around the figure without one, and `fold_cutting` is how a cartridge says so. The absence is a construction choice, not a missing interface.
