Term

kirigami

Definition

Paper folded AND cut: slits and cut-outs made in a sheet so that it opens, stretches or stands up in ways folding alone cannot reach. The word joins the Japanese kiru (to cut) and kami (paper). In engineering it names cut patterns that make a flat sheet auxetic or spring-like.
