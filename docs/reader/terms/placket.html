Term

placket

Definition

A finished opening in a garment — the slit that lets a head, a hand or a body through, faced or bound so it can be closed again. Where a button stand is the extension a closure sits on, the placket is the opening itself: the two edges, which one laps over which, and how far.

carcela

Definição

Uma abertura acabada numa peça — a fenda que deixa passar uma cabeça, uma mão ou um corpo, entretelada ou debruada para poder voltar a fechar-se. Onde a carcela de botões é o prolongamento em que assenta o fecho, a carcela de abertura é a própria abertura: os dois bordos, qual monta sobre qual, e até onde.

Restricción

The two edges of a placket are sewn to a body that has to close: the underlap must carry an edge of exactly the overlap's length, measured off the drawn curve rather than computed twice — fashion-cabinet/changpao draws its dajin once and marks the underpanel from the SAME curve, so the two are equal by construction.
