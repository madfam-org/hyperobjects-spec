Definição

Restricción

The opening must clear the widest point ON THE WAY IN, which is rarely the point it will sit on: a waistband that fits the waist has to pass the hip, and a neck opening that fits the neck has to pass the head — `head_girth` appears on 30 cartridges for exactly this reason.
