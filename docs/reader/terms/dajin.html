dajin (cierre diagonal)

Definición

El delantero diagonal y montado de las tradiciones de túnica china — la curva que va del cuello, cruzando el pecho, hasta la axila, cerrada con alamares anudados a lo largo de su canto. Term

dajin (diagonal front closure)

Definition

The diagonal overlapping front of the Chinese robe traditions — the curve that runs from the neck across the chest to the underarm, closed with knotted frog fastenings along its edge. It is the structural signature the changpao shares with the qipao, and it is a single drawn curve rather than a straight lap: biased outward near the neck and downward near the underarm.

Termo

dajin (fecho diagonal)

Definição

A frente diagonal sobreposta das tradições de túnica chinesa — a curva que vai do pescoço, atravessando o peito, até à axila, fechada com alamares atados ao longo do seu bordo. É a assinatura estrutural que o changpao partilha com o qipao, e é uma única curva desenhada e não um cruzamento direito: enviesada para fora junto ao pescoço e para baixo junto à axila.

Restricción

The panel underneath must carry an edge of EXACTLY the dajin's length or the overlap will not lie flat and the whole front twists. fashion-cabinet/changpao draws the curve once, measures its length off the drawn edge, and marks the underpanel's seat from the same curve; the frogs are then spaced along the measured ARC rather than the chord.
