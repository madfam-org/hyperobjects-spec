Term

thread

Definition

A CDG interface type: a helical ridge that converts rotation into axial travel and clamping force, letting two parts join and separate repeatedly without a fastener. Threads are the commons' most standardized interface — a cartridge that declares a real designation (M22×1, 1/4-20 UNC, PCO 1881) mates with hardware nobody in the commons had to draw.

Termo

rosca

Definição

Tipo de interface CDG: um filete helicoidal que converte a rotação em avanço axial e força de aperto, permitindo que duas peças se unam e se separem repetidamente sem ferragem adicional. As roscas são a interface mais normalizada do comum: um cartucho que declara uma designação real (M22×1, 1/4-20 UNC, PCO 1881) acopla com ferragens que ninguém no comum teve de desenhar.

Restricción

A printed thread needs a clearance parameter of its own: the designation fixes the nominal geometry, and a cartridge that prints to nominal binds in every material that shrinks — which is why the commons' thread cartridges pair a thread_std with a clearance rather than a single dimension.
