Term

standard part

Definition

A commercial off-the-shelf part in the keystone's catalog — a stepper motor, an extrusion, a bearing, a propeller — described by facts only: its governing standard or datasheet with citations, its dimensions, optional parameters such as a cut length, and its mating interfaces. Assemblies reference it by key; no CAD of it is stored.
