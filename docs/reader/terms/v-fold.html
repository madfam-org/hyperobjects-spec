Term

V-fold

Definition

The basic angled pop-up: a folded piece glued across the gutter with its two glue lines at an angle to it, so opening the spread pulls the lines apart and the piece rises. The angle sets how far it leans and how high it stands.

Termo

dobra em V

Definição

O pop-up angular básico: uma peça dobrada colada atravessando a dobra, com as duas linhas de colagem em ângulo em relação a ela, de modo que ao abrir a página dupla as linhas se afastam e a peça se levanta. O ângulo define quanto se inclina e que altura atinge.

Restricción

The piece's own fold, its two glue lines and the gutter must meet at one point; a piece whose fold misses that point cannot fold flat and either buckles or tears its tabs when the spread closes.
