Term

flat-foldability

Definition

Whether a crease pattern can be folded all the way flat, every crease at ±180°, without the paper stretching, tearing or passing through itself. At one vertex it is decided by Maekawa's and Kawasaki's conditions; for a whole pattern it also depends on the stacking order of the layers.

Termo

dobrabilidade plana

Definição

Se um padrão de vincos pode ser dobrado até ficar completamente plano, com cada vinco a ±180°, sem que o papel estique, rasgue ou se atravesse a si mesmo. Num vértice decidem-no as condições de Maekawa e de Kawasaki; num padrão inteiro depende também da ordem em que as camadas se empilham.

Restricción

Local conditions are necessary, not sufficient: a pattern whose every vertex passes Maekawa and Kawasaki can still be impossible to fold flat because of layer order, and deciding global flat-foldability is NP-hard. Never report a pattern flat-foldable from vertex checks alone.
