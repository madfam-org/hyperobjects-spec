Term

alforza (horizontal hem tuck)

Definition

One of the horizontal tucks running around a pollera near its hem — the full gathered skirt of Aymara and Quechua women of the Bolivian and Peruvian altiplano. The tucks ornament the skirt and are practical at the same time: letting them out lengthens the pollera as it is passed down or as fashion shifts.

Termo

alforza (prega horizontal de bainha)

Definição

Uma das pregas horizontais que percorrem uma pollera junto à bainha — a saia ampla e franzida das mulheres aimarás e quíchuas do altiplano boliviano e peruano. As pregas ornamentam a saia e são ao mesmo tempo práticas: desfazê-las alonga a pollera quando é passada adiante ou quando a moda muda.

Restricción

Each tuck eats twice its depth in cloth, so the cut length must include the take-up: `CUT_LENGTH = finished_length + alforza_count * alforza_depth * 2 + hem_allowance`. A draft that adds the tucks after solving the length delivers a skirt short by exactly the take-up.
