Term

pleat

Definition

Fullness folded into the cloth and fixed at one edge, so that a wide flat panel becomes a narrow finished one that opens again when the body moves. A pleat differs from a gather in that it is drawn: its depth, its pitch and its count are decided on the pattern, and the cloth it consumes is the take-up those three imply.

Termo

prega

Definição

Amplitude dobrada dentro do tecido e fixada num bordo, de modo que um painel plano e largo se torna um painel acabado e estreito que volta a abrir quando o corpo se move. Uma prega distingue-se de um franzido por ser traçada: a sua profundidade, o seu passo e a sua contagem decidem-se no molde, e o tecido que consome é o embebido que esses três implicam.

Restricción

The take-up is a multiple of the pleat depth fixed by the pleat's type — two depths for a knife pleat, four for a box pleat — so the count solves as an INTEGER against the flat width and the finished width together. Rounding the count after both widths are fixed spends the whole rounding error on the last pleat, which is exactly where it shows.
