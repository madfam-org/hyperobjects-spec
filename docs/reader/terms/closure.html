Term

closure

Definition

The place where a garment opens to be put on and closes to be worn, taken as one interface regardless of what fastens it. Naming the region separately from the fastening is what lets a cartridge change from hooks to snaps to magnets without redrafting the opening, and it is where the accessibility of a garment actually lives.

Termo

fecho

Definição

O lugar onde uma peça se abre para ser vestida e fecha para ser usada, tomado como uma só interface seja qual for o que a prende. Nomear a região à parte do fecho concreto é o que permite a um cartucho passar de colchetes a molas ou a ímanes sem voltar a traçar a abertura, e é onde reside de facto a acessibilidade de uma peça.

Restricción

The two edges that meet are sewn to a body that has to close, so they are equal in length and their lap direction is a stated fact — an opening whose overlap is left to the maker is an opening that will be made both ways and fit one of them.
