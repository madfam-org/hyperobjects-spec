Term

leporello (accordion book)

Definition

A book made of one long strip folded back and forth in alternate mountain and valley folds, so it opens page by page or pulls out into one continuous panorama. The name recalls Leporello, the servant in Mozart's Don Giovanni whose catalogue of his master's conquests unrolls at length.

Termo

leporello (livro sanfona)

Definição

Um livro feito de uma só tira comprida dobrada em ziguezague, com dobras alternadas em montanha e em vale, que se abre página a página ou se estende num panorama contínuo. O nome lembra Leporello, o criado do Don Giovanni de Mozart cujo catálogo das conquistas do amo se desenrola longamente.

Restricción

Panels must be equal in width (or deliberately graded) for the strip to stack square when closed; a strip longer than one sheet is joined with a glue flap on a valley fold, where the joint hides inside the closed book.
