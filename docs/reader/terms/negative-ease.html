English

generated written by a drafting pass, not natively reviewed

Term

negative ease

Definition

Ease with the sign reversed: the finished garment measures LESS than the body, and the difference is taken up by the fabric stretching over it. It is expressed as a percentage rather than as millimetres because what the cloth can give is proportional to how much cloth there is.

Português

generated redigido por uma passagem de rascunho, sem revisão nativa

Termo

folga negativa

Definição

Folga com o sinal invertido: a peça acabada mede MENOS do que o corpo, e a diferença é absorvida pelo tecido ao esticar sobre ele. Exprime-se em percentagem e não em milímetros porque o que o tecido pode dar é proporcional à quantidade de tecido.

Restricción

The percentage must be spent against a fabric that has it, and never twice: a garment drafted at 15% negative ease in a cloth measured at 15% stretch has used the cloth's whole range, so the pattern is at the limit of what any body can put on.
