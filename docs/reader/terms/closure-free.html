Definition

The capability of a garment that stays on the body with no fastening at all — wrapped, tied, or simply wide enough to pass. It is a whole design tradition rather than an omission: a garment with nothing to break, nothing to source, and nothing to fit but the cloth.

Termo

sem fecho

Definição

A capacidade de uma peça que se mantém no corpo sem qualquer fecho — enrolada, atada, ou simplesmente larga o bastante para passar. É toda uma tradição de desenho e não uma falta: uma peça sem nada que parta, nada que seja preciso arranjar e nada a ajustar além do tecido.

Restricción

With no fastening, every dimension that would have been adjustable is now fixed at draft time: the opening must clear the widest part it has to pass, and the wrap must have enough overlap to stay closed in movement rather than at rest.
