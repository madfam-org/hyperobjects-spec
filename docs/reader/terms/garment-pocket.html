Term

pocket

Definition

An opening in a garment with a bag behind it. The interface is really the mouth: where it is, how wide it is, and which edge of which piece it is cut into. The bag is consequence — it hangs from the mouth, and everything it can hold has to pass through it first.

Termo

bolso

Definição

Uma abertura numa peça com um saco por trás. A interface é, na verdade, a boca: onde está, quanto mede e em que bordo de que peça está aberta. O saco é consequência — pende da boca, e tudo o que pode conter tem primeiro de passar por ela.

Restricción

The mouth is the dimension that matters, and it is a dimension about a hand: a bag wider than its own opening is usable, a bag narrower than its opening is a bag whose corners nothing reaches.
