Notas de compensación

English

Name

Cotton jersey (single knit)

Compensation notes

Weft (course) direction carries the stretch — garments are cut with the stretch running around the body. Negative ease up to ~10% is comfortable for this blend; tubular shrinkage favors pre-washing production yardage.
