Societal benefit

Commercial precision nozzles are sold pre-set to one orifice and one length, and are thrown away with the bottle. A printed 410 nozzle lets anyone match the tip exactly to the task — a fine needle for glue, a controlled drip for dosing, a cuttable cone for sealants — and refit it to any refill bottle, turning a disposable dispenser into a precise, reusable tool.
