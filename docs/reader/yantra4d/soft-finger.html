Beneficio social

Los dedos neumaticos blandos agarran objetos fragiles o irregulares que los pinzas rigidas aplastan — fruta, tejido, muestras de laboratorio. Name

Soft Pneumatic Finger

Societal benefit

Soft pneumatic fingers grip fragile or irregular objects that rigid grippers crush — fruit, tissue, lab samples. Commercial soft actuators are costly and closed; an open, parametric finger with a standard 4/6 mm air port lets a lab or maker cast or print a gripper to size and repair it in the field, lowering the barrier to soft-robotics research and assistive devices.
