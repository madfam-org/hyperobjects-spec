Societal benefit

Electrical retrofit is full of boxes that sit too deep, mount to crumbling plaster, or lack a fixing point after a remodel. Printable ears, standoffs and mud rings on the standard #6-32 device pattern let an installer or DIYer salvage an existing box — bring it flush to a new wall finish, lift it off a rough surface, or add a wall flange — instead of cutting a new opening, using code-standard geometry.
