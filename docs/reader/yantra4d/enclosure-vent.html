Societal benefit

A hot 3D printer, PC, or project box needs airflow, but a vent panel sized to your exact cutout with the open area and shielding you want is a bespoke part. A parametric louver, hex grille, or slot vent on a screw-mount border lets anyone fit ventilation to any enclosure opening — trading open area for sight/weather shielding as needed — instead of drilling holes or leaving electronics to cook.
