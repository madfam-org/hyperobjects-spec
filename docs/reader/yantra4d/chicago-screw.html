Name

Chicago Screw

Societal benefit

A Chicago screw is the difference between a strap that can be shortened, re-holed and re-used and one that is riveted shut for good. Printing them to an existing punched hole size means a strap outlives the bag it came on, and a repair shop stops needing a wall of size-specific brass hardware to take in one job.
