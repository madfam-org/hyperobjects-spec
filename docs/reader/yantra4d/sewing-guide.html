Sewing Machine Foot / Guide

Societal benefit

Straight, even seam allowances are the difference between a garment that fits and one that doesn't, yet branded seam guides and specialty feet are sold per machine and per task. Low/high shank is a near-universal split across household machines, so a printed shank-clamp guide or a plate-bolted seam fence gives any sewist an adjustable, repeatable seam allowance without buying a foot for every width.
