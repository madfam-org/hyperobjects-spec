Window / Sash Lock Insert

Societal benefit

Window sash locks and vent stops are frequently lost or broken, and replacements are hard to source for older or imported windows — leaving windows insecure or unable to be safely vented around children. A parametric insert lets anyone refit a window with a snap catch, a child-safety vent stop, or a friction wedge, restoring both security and safe ventilation cheaply.
