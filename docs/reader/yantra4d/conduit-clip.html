Societal benefit

Neat, code-adjacent conduit support is often the missing piece in DIY and small-shop wiring; a strip of printed clips sized to the exact tube OD lets anyone dress a run cleanly instead of leaving cable draped or stapled. Standoff and gang variants keep runs off hot surfaces and organize multi-circuit routes, improving safety and serviceability for off-grid, workshop and outbuilding installs.
