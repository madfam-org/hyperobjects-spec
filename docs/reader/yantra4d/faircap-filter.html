Societal benefit

A discarded PET bottle plus a printed cap becomes a working water filter. Anyone with a printer and a filter medium can make safe drinking water from questionable sources — no proprietary cartridges, no supply chain, no commercial dependency. Water sovereignty and plastic upcycling in one object; the output side of the bottle-thread ecosystem.
