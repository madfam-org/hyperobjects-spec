Societal benefit

Hobby servos are the cheapest actuators in robotics, but the mounting hardware to point them is rarely in the box. A printed bracket cut to the SG90/MG996R body and flange lets any of these interchangeable servos build a pan/tilt head, a gripper joint or a robot limb from parts on hand.
