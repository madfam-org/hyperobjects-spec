Beneficio social

Una varilla de ferro es la fuente de fuego más fiable sin llama abierta, pero las varillas desnudas no tienen agarre y se caen o se pierden con las manos frías. Name

Firestarter / Ferro Handle

Societal benefit

A ferro rod is the most reliable open-flame-free fire source, but bare rods have no grip and are dropped or lost with cold hands. A printable handle sized to the 6 mm or 8 mm rod standard restores control and stores the rod with its striker, so a fire kit stays usable and repairable anywhere without buying a proprietary handle.
