Societal benefit

Metal purse frames are sold in a handful of fixed widths, so a maker either redraws the whole bag pattern to fit the frame or abandons the design; printing the frame to the pattern's own mouth width flips that dependency and keeps small leather and textile workshops off a narrow import supply chain.
