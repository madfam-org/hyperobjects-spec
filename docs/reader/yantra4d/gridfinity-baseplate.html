Societal benefit

Gridfinity is the open, community-owned 42 mm storage grid that any bin, tray or holder can share. A printed screw-down or magnet baseplate anchors a Gridfinity field to any drawer, bench or toolbox on demand, so a whole shop's small parts stay organised and reconfigurable using freely-licensed geometry instead of a proprietary drawer system.
