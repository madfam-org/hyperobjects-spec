5/8in Spigot Adapter

Societal benefit

The 5/8" baby pin is the universal grip and lighting interface — every stand, C-stand and grip head accepts it, and studio devices carry 1/4-20 or 3/8-16 threads. On-demand spigot adapters let a crew mate any light, flag or accessory to any stand from printed parts, instead of hunting for the exact adapter, and replace a stripped or lost spigot on set.
