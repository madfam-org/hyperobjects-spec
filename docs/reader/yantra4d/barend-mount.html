Motorcycle Bar-End / Mirror Mount

Societal benefit

Handlebar accessory mounts are proprietary and priced per accessory, yet nearly every bar in the world is one of a handful of diameters (22.2 / 25.4 / 31.8 mm). A clamp printed to the exact bar — plus a bar-end plug for the hollow tube — lets a rider fit a mirror, phone or camera to any motorcycle or bicycle from durable printed parts, and standardise every mount on the bike to one bolt size instead of buying a per-brand bracket.
