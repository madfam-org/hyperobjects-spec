Beneficio social

La cola de milano Arca-Swiss de 38 mm es la liberación rápida de trípode abierta de facto — placas, mordazas y soportes en L de docenas de fabricantes interoperan en el mismo perfil. Societal benefit

The Arca-Swiss 38 mm dovetail is the de-facto open tripod quick-release — plates, clamps and L-brackets from dozens of makers all interoperate on the same profile. On-demand Arca plates and clamps let a photographer fit any camera, lens foot or accessory to any head from printed parts, instead of buying a proprietary plate per body, and repair a lost or broken plate in the field.
