Beneficio social

Pneumatic Bellows Actuator

Societal benefit

Every soft-robotics build starts with a bellows, and every tutorial starts by telling you to mould one in silicone — a two-day process needing a mould, a vacuum chamber, and a material with a shelf life. A printed convoluted bellows collapses that to an afternoon on a machine a school or clinic already owns, and because its stroke axis and inlet are published interfaces, the same part serves as a lift, a gripper drive, and a valve actuator instead of being a one-off.
