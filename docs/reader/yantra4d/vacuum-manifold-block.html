Name

Vacuum Manifold Block

Societal benefit

A soft-pneumatic rig is only as useful as its plumbing. Commercial vacuum manifolds are sold per-port at machine-shop prices, which is why teaching labs and small pick-and-place builds end up with a tangle of tees. An open manifold whose port pitch and barb series are published as interfaces lets a lab print exactly the number of outlets it needs, at the pitch its cup array already uses, and know it will mate the rest of the family without measuring anything.
