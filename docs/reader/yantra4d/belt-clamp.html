Name

Timing Belt Clamp

Societal benefit

A snapped timing belt or a loose one usually means buying a whole new closed-loop belt. A printed tooth-matched clamp terminates open belt stock to any length, splices a broken loop, or tensions a slack run — keeping printers, plotters and CoreXY robots moving from a reel of raw belt.
