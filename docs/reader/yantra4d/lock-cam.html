Name

Cabinet Cam-Lock Cam

Societal benefit

Cam-lock cams are a frequent point of failure and are rarely stocked as spares — a snapped cam usually forces replacement of the whole lock or cabinet. A parametric cam sized to the 16/19 mm standards lets anyone reprint the exact catch geometry, keeping mailboxes, lockers, tool chests and cabinets in service instead of landfilled.
