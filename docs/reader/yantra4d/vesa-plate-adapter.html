Name

VESA Adapter Plate (75<->100)

Societal benefit

The VESA FDMI flat-display mounting interface is the universal open standard for monitor and TV mounting, but the 75 mm and 100 mm patterns rarely match between a display and its arm. A printed adapter plate reconciles the two on demand, so any monitor mounts to any arm without buying a proprietary bracket, and rescues an out-of-production mount in minutes.
