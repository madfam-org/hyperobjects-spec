Magnetic Button Cover

Societal benefit

Adaptive magnetic shirts are sold as whole garments at several times the price of the shirt someone already owns, and converting a shirt by replacing its buttons alters it permanently — impossible for a uniform, a school shirt, or something borrowed or inherited. A cap that snaps on and a plate that stitches behind the buttonhole convert any placket reversibly, in minutes, and move to the next shirt when that one wears out.
