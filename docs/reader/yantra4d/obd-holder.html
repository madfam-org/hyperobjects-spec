Beneficio social

Un dongle OBD-II colgando y fusibles de repuesto sueltos son un peligro en el área de los pies y una pieza perdida en potencia. OBD-II / Fuse Tap Holder

Societal benefit

A dangling OBD-II dongle and loose spare fuses are a footwell hazard and a lost-part waiting to happen. A cradle sized to the exact dongle and a fuse box sized to the ISO blade-fuse footprints tidy a vehicle's diagnostic and electrical kit from printed parts, extend the useful life of a scan tool, and standardise on the same OBD-II connector geometry every car since 1996 already shares.
