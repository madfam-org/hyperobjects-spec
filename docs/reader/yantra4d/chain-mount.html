Name

Cable Drag-Chain Mount

Societal benefit

A cable drag chain protects the wiring on any moving axis, but its end brackets are vendor-specific and easily lost. A printed mount that presents the chain-end bolt holes and its own surface / extrusion mounting lets any drag-chain run be anchored on a printer, CNC or robot from parts on hand.
