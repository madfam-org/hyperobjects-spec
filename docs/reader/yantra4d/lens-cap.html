Societal benefit

The photographic filter thread is a decades-old open standard shared by every lens front and screw-in filter. Lost lens caps and missing step rings are a constant, wasteful expense; on-demand caps and step rings let a photographer replace a lost cap or bridge two filter sizes for pennies of filament, keeping filters and lenses in use instead of buying single-purpose accessories.
