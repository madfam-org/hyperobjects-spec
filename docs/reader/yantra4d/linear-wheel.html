Societal benefit

V-wheels are the rolling interface of the OpenBuilds motion ecosystem used in CNC routers, laser cutters, and 3D printers. A printable V-wheel at the correct 45° profile keeps a gantry rolling when a wheel wears out or a rail needs a custom diameter.
