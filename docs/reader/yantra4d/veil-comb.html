Name

Veil Comb

Societal benefit

Veil attachment is the part of bridal millinery that nobody sells a tool for: the gather is done by eye onto a plain plastic comb, and if it comes out uneven the veil is remade. A comb whose spine states the gather pitch turns a craft secret into a measurement, and makes a veil a thing a person can attach, detach and reattach to a different comb across a lifetime.
