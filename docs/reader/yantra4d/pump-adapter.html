Societal benefit

A working pump on an empty bottle is landfill the moment its refill uses a different neck. Threading to the real SPI 410 finishes lets anyone move a pump between bottles, adapt a 28-410 pump onto a 24-410 refill, or seal a dispenser for travel — keeping pumps and bottles in service across brands instead of discarding a whole dispenser per refill mismatch.
