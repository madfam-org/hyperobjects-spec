Name

Reacher / Button-Hook Dressing Aid

Societal benefit

Buttoning a shirt, closing a jacket, and pulling on socks are private, daily acts of independence that stroke, arthritis, a single working hand, or limited reach can quietly end — often forcing a wait for a carer. Printed dressing aids sized to the user's hand and clothing restore self-dressing at near-zero cost, and can be re-shaped and reprinted as ability changes, keeping a basic dignity in the user's own hands.
