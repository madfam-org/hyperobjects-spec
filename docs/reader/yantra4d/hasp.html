Name

Padlock Hasp & Shackle Guard

Societal benefit

A hasp plus any padlock is the most accessible lock there is, but a printed hasp lets people secure sheds, gates, tool chests, animal enclosures and disaster-relief storage where buying hardware is impractical. The shackle guard adds bolt-cutter resistance the cheapest commercial hasps lack, raising security for those who cannot afford a hardened lock.
