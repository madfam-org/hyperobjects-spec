Societal benefit

The 8 mm stud grid is the most widely owned construction toy interface on Earth. Printable brick-compatible plates, tiles and adapters let families repair, extend and remix builds — replacing a lost baseplate, mounting a build to a wall, or blanking a tile for custom lettering — from open files, keeping expensive sets in play instead of landfill and letting learners design their own compatible parts.
