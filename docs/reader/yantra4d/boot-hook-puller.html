Name

Boot Hook Puller

Societal benefit

A tall boot's pull loop is useless without something to hook it with, and commercial boot-pulls are sold as a curiosity rather than a tool. Printing a pair sized to the actual loop lets someone get their own work boots or waders on standing up, without a second person and without wrecking the loop by clawing at it with their fingers.
