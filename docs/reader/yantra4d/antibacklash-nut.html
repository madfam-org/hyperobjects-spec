Name

Lead Screw Anti-Backlash Nut

Societal benefit

Backlash is the enemy of precision: a worn or loose lead-screw nut turns a printer or CNC into a dimension lottery. A printed T8 anti-backlash nut with a preload spring restores lost-motion-free travel for the cost of a few grams of filament, replacing a proprietary metal nut that can cost more than the machine's screw. It is the canonical repair part for the ubiquitous T8 Z axis, and the tuned `clearance` lets any printer make a nut that fits its own screw.
