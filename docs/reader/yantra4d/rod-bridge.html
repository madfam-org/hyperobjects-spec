Beneficio social

El sistema de varillas LWS de 15 mm es la columna vertebral abierta del rigging de cámara, pero las placas puente y superiores son piezas mecanizadas caras. 15mm Rod Bridge Plate

Societal benefit

The 15 mm LWS rod system is the open backbone of camera rigging, but bridge plates and top plates are pricey machined parts. A printable rod bridge that clamps both rails at the 60 mm standard — as a cheese plate, a riser, or an upright face — lets an owner-operator build the exact accessory platform a shoot needs and repair a rig in the field. Sharing the 15 mm bore, it rides the same rails as any rod clamp and grows the cinema-rod family.
