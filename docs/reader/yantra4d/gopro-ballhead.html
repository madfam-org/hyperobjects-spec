Beneficio social

Las cámaras de acción se montan rígidas por defecto; añadir un eje de inclinación suele implicar comprar una rótula propietaria. Name

GoPro Ball-Head Mount

Societal benefit

Action cameras mount rigidly by default; adding a tilt axis usually means buying a proprietary ball head. A printable ball-and-socket that speaks the GoPro finger language lets a creator drop an articulating joint anywhere in a GoPro rig — angle the camera, pinch to lock — and screw a ball onto any tripod with the 1/4-20 variant. It gives the gopro-mount family an angle-adjustable companion.
