Beneficio social

Las prendas con electrónica casi siempre mueren en la salida del cable: la funda se flexiona, la soldadura se fatiga y se desecha toda la prenda por una unión de dos centavos. Name

Seam Strain Relief

Societal benefit

Wearable-electronics garments almost always die at the cable exit: the jacket flexes, the solder joint fatigues, and the whole garment is scrapped over a two-cent joint. A printed seam clamp moves that load onto the stitch line and makes the failure repairable at a kitchen table instead of a repair depot.
