Name

TPU Lattice Panel

Societal benefit

A flexible protective lattice anyone can print turns scale armor and impact-protective wear from a specialist craft into an open, made-to-measure pattern: rigid tiles where you need protection, thin bridges where you need to move. Printed in TPU it is washable, repairable tile-by-tile, and recyclable; sized to a garment panel it is a fabric that is simultaneously a Fashion Cabinet cloth and a Yantra4D solid.
