15mm Rod Rig Clamp

Societal benefit

The 15 mm LWS rod system is the open backbone of professional and indie filmmaking — every matte box, follow-focus and monitor clamps to the same 15 mm rods at 60 mm spacing. On-demand clamps, bridges and risers let a filmmaker build exactly the rig they need from stock rod and printed parts, instead of buying expensive single-purpose brackets, and repair a cracked clamp on location.
