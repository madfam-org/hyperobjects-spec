Heel Tip Blank

Societal benefit

Heel tips are the one component of a shoe designed to be consumed, yet their sizes are a manufacturer's private grid — so a pair of otherwise sound shoes is thrown out because nobody stocks the tip. A parametric blank makes the wearing part of a heel a printable consumable again, which is the difference between a shoe that lasts a decade and one that lasts a season.
