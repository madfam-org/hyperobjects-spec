Drill Press Fence

Societal benefit

A drill-press fence with a flip stop turns a bare drill table into a repeatable boring jig — shelf-pin rows, dowel joints, hardware patterns all land on the mark. Printed on demand, it fits any drill or router table with 3/4 in T-track, and the flip stop and mount bracket reprint in minutes if lost. It clamps to the same track as bench dogs and featherboards, growing the miter/T-track commons.
