Beneficio social

Cada eje de impresora 3D, CNC y robot necesita su endstop sujeto en el punto exacto, y el soporte original rara vez encaja en un marco modificado. Societal benefit

Every 3D printer, CNC and robot axis needs its endstop held at exactly the right spot, and the OEM bracket rarely fits a modified frame. A printed mount cut to the standard Omron microswitch footprint, with adjustment slots, lets any salvaged limit switch home a machine precisely.
