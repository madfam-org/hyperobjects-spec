Modular Grid Interoperability Hub

Societal benefit

The maker world has fractured into rival organization grids — Gridfinity for drawers, Multiboard and pegboard for walls, French cleats for tools — and a bin or holder built for one will not mount on another, forcing a rebuild per system. As the Commons' #300 keystone, this hub is the single adapter that bridges them: it lets a Gridfinity bin hang on a cleat wall, a pegboard carry a Gridfinity cell, or a Multiboard meet Gridfinity — so the whole catalog of parts becomes mountable anywhere, and no one is locked into a single grid.
