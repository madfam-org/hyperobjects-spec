DIN Rail Terminal Comb

Societal benefit

DIN rail is the universal open backbone of electrical control panels and consumer units worldwide, and terminal-block accessories — jumpers, end stops, marker strips — are proprietary, per-brand and easily lost. A printed comb, end bracket or marker carrier at the correct terminal pitch keeps a panel serviceable and re-labellable with commodity parts instead of ordering a manufacturer-specific accessory.
