Societal benefit

Exposed terminals and busbars are a live-contact and debris hazard in field panels, junction boxes and hobby power builds. Printed hoods, IP20 comb guards and end barriers sized to DIN rail and block pitch restore touch-safe finger protection where the original covers are lost or were never fitted, making home, workshop and off-grid electrical work safer and tidier.
