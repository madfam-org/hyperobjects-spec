Societal benefit

Independent dressing is one of the first things lost to arthritis, hemiparesis or reduced pinch strength, and a zipper is often the specific barrier. Commercial zipper aids are sold at assistive-device prices for what is a few grams of plastic. A clamp on the slider body multiplies the available hand force by the length of the arm, and because the geometry is parameterised, it can be fitted to the user's own zipper and their own hand rather than to an average of both.
