Societal benefit

3/4 in T-track is the shared standard of shop jigs, fences, sleds and drill tables. Printed hold-downs, step blocks and push stops let a woodworker fixture any workpiece to any track-equipped jig without buying a proprietary clamp set, and print a replacement the moment one cracks. They ride the same track as bench dogs and featherboards, growing the miter/T-track commons.
