Beneficio social

Las transmisiones pequeñas son una Torre de Babel de perfiles de eje — ejes D de motor de 5 mm, encoders redondos de 6 mm, puntas hexagonales de 1/4 in — y una incompatibilidad normalmente implica un acoplador mecanizado caro o abandonar una reparación. Name

Shaft Coupler Kit

Societal benefit

Small drivetrains are a Tower of Babel of shaft profiles — 5 mm D motor shafts, 6 mm round encoders, 1/4 in hex bits — and a mismatch normally means an expensive machined coupler or abandoning a repair. A printable any-to-any coupler with independently selectable round/D/hex bores lets a maker join whatever two shafts they have, or step a worn bore down to a fresh one, keeping motors, knobs and appliances in service.
