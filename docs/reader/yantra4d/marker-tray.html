Societal benefit

A magnet-mounted marker tray sized to the actual marker keeps a classroom or meeting-room whiteboard organized without adhesive trays that fall off, and prints to fit whatever markers are on hand — a low-cost fix for schools that replaces a consumable plastic accessory with a one-time print.
