Robot Wheel / Hub

Societal benefit

Small robots run on a handful of motor shaft standards — 3/4 mm D-shafts, 6 mm round, and the ubiquitous TT gearmotor. A printed wheel or hub keyed to the exact shaft turns any of those motors into drive without buying a matching wheel, and the bore-clearance control tunes the press fit per printer.
