Societal benefit

Disposable gel electrodes are thrown away by the sheet every session, and reusable sensing garments are sold as sealed products nobody can repair. A printed carrier lets a clinic, a lab, or a person at home mount a standard snap stud on washable conductive fabric and replace only the patch, turning a consumable into a serviceable garment.
