Name

Bottle-to-Bottle Coupler

Societal benefit

A coupler turns two abundant PET bottles into a bigger closed system with no new vessel: invert-and-store to save fridge space, run the tornado-tube vortex as a free science demonstration, or decant through the funnel end. All from bottles that would otherwise be waste, with a printed part that costs a few grams of filament.
