Name

Pneumatic Barb Port

Societal benefit

Soft-robotics tutorials assume a drawer of commercial push-to-connect fittings that costs more than the actuator it feeds and is unobtainable in much of the world. A barb port parameterised on tube inner diameter turns any tubing on hand into a working pneumatic connection, and — because every actuator in this family speaks the same barb series — makes six otherwise isolated printable objects into one interoperable kit.
