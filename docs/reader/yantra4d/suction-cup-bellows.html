Societal benefit

Vacuum cups are consumables: the lip abrades, the bellows cracks, and a replacement is a proprietary part number with a minimum order quantity. A parametric cup printed in TPU restores a stalled pick-and-place line, a lab automation rig, or a school robotics project the same day, at a cup diameter chosen for the part actually being picked rather than for whatever the supplier still stocks.
