Beneficio social

Name

Peristaltic Pump Rotor

Societal benefit

Peristaltic pumps dose fluids without contamination because the pump never touches the liquid — only the disposable tube does, which is why they run dialysis, IV lines, lab reagents and aquaria. Commercial pump heads are proprietary and priced per tube size; an open, parametric rotor and race sized for standard 3/16 and 1/4 in tubing let a lab or maker build a dosing pump to the flow they need and swap the tube instead of the pump.
