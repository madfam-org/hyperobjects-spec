Name

Wide-Mouth Jar Adapter

Societal benefit

The mason jar and the PET bottle are the two most abundant standardized household vessels, but their threads never met. This adapter bridges them: a jar becomes a refill reservoir for any PCO-1881 bottle system, and the plain and sifter lids replace lost or rusted metal mason lids on demand — one printed part unlocking the whole cross-family ecosystem.
