Societal benefit

The same cheap sensor breakouts — a VL53L0X rangefinder, an MPU6050 IMU, a Pi camera — show up in every hobby robot, yet each needs holding at a specific angle to see. A printed mount cut to the board's exact hole pattern, with a tilt slot, aims any of them without a custom bracket per build.
