Name

Drip Irrigation Manifold

Societal benefit

Micro-drip irrigation saves water by delivering it at the root, but off-the-shelf manifolds come in fixed outlet counts and layouts. A printable manifold sized to the exact number of drip lines a bed needs — inline, radial, or a simple wye — lets a gardener or smallholder branch a watering system on demand from a single 1/4 in barb standard, and repair a cracked distributor in the field instead of replacing the whole run.
