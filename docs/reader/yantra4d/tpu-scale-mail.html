Beneficio social

Una tela de cota de escamas que cualquiera puede imprimir convierte la armadura de escamas de dragón y la escamería expresiva — históricamente un ensamble manual laborioso — en un patrón abierto a la medida: escamas que articulan de forma independiente, que protegen y se mueven. Name

TPU Scale Mail

Societal benefit

A scale-mail fabric anyone can print turns dragon-scale armor and expressive scalework — historically painstaking hand assembly — into an open, made-to-measure pattern: independently articulating scales that protect and move. Printed in TPU it is washable, repairable scale-by-scale, and recyclable; sized to a garment panel it is a fabric that is simultaneously a Fashion Cabinet cloth and a Yantra4D solid.
