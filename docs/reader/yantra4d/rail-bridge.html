Name

Rail Standard Bridge

Societal benefit

Picatinny, NATO and Arca-Swiss rails each dominate a different world — optics/firearms, tactical accessories, and photography — and none of them mate. A printed double-sided bridge lets a photographer clamp an Arca head onto a NATO rail, or a maker mount a Picatinny accessory to a tripod plate, without buying a proprietary cross-adapter for every pairing. One open block collapses three incompatible ecosystems into an interoperable one.
