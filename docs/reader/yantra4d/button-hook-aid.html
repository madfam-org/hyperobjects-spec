Button Hook Aid

Societal benefit

Buttoning aids are sold in one size with a handle nobody with arthritis can hold and a wire loop sized for shirt buttons only, so people give up on buttoned clothing and buy pull-on garments instead. A hook whose loop matches the buttons on the coat already in the closet, on a handle matched to the hand that has to hold it, keeps someone dressing themselves.
