Name

Crochet / Knitting Needle Gauge

Societal benefit

Knitting needles and crochet hooks lose their printed size the moment they leave the packet, and US/metric labelling is inconsistent across brands. A sizing gauge whose holes are the real metric ladder (2.0 mm/US0 through 12.0 mm/US17) restores the size of any orphan needle to one shared standard. Printed on demand, it replaces a bought metal gauge and can be tuned so holes measure true on the maker's own printer.
