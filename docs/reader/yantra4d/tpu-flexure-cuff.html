Societal benefit

A stretch cuff anyone can print replaces sewn elastic — which perishes and is rarely repairable — with an open, made-to-measure flexure band that is washable, recyclable, and springs back for the life of the garment. Sized to a sleeve or hem it is a trim that is simultaneously a Fashion Cabinet notion and a Yantra4D solid.
